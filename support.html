Cawing NAM Support

Cawing NAM is a Neural Amp Modeler player for macOS (standalone app and Logic / GarageBand Audio Unit).

Contact

Use the support link on the Cawing NAM Mac App Store product page, or email the address you publish in App Store Connect (see the submission guide). Please include your macOS version, audio interface, and whether you are using the standalone app or the plugin.

Quick troubleshooting

  • No sound: Click Engage, allow microphone access in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone, and confirm the Audio tab input/output devices.
  • Empty library: Choose File → Import NAM Profiles… and select .nam files. TONE3000 sign-in is optional.
  • Plugin missing in Logic: Launch Cawing NAM once from Applications, then rescan Audio Units in Logic.
  • High latency: Use an audio interface, buffer 64–128, and avoid Bluetooth I/O.

TONE3000

TONE3000 is an optional third-party catalog. Account, search, and download issues on that service are handled by TONE3000. Report a community capture on tone3000.com.
